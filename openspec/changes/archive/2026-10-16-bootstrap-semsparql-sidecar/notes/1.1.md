## Exported-Surface Ownership Check ("Before adding anything new")

1. **Who owns this responsibility today?**
   - Forward entity-to-IRI mapping and N-Triples object serialization are owned by
     `github.com/c360studio/semengine/vocabulary/export` (`export.SubjectIRI`, `export.SerializeToString`).
     `irimap` delegates forward IRI formatting and N-Triples object serialization directly to `export` without
     reimplementing either rule.
   - Six-part entity ID parsing and validation are owned by `github.com/c360studio/semengine/pkg/types`
     (`types.ValidateEntityID`, `types.ParseEntityID`).
   - Inverse IRI-to-entity-ID mapping and deterministic reification blank-node hashing have no upstream owner
     (SemEngine is write/export-only and does not invert entity IRIs or fold/unfold SPARQL BGPs).
2. **Is the premise true?**
   - Confirmed in `openspec/changes/bootstrap-semsparql-sidecar/inventory.md` and `internal/harness/contract/probes_test.go`.
3. **Who is it for?**
   - `ErrNotEntityIRI`, `Mapper`, `NewMapper`, `(*Mapper).BaseIRI`, `(*Mapper).EntityIDToIRI`,
     `(*Mapper).IRIToEntityID`: consumed by `sparql` BGP planner/evaluator (Tasks 2.2, 3.1–3.3) and `server`
     `/sparql` + `/export` handlers (Tasks 2.3, 3.3); specified in `design.md` ("Responsibilities and consumer
     surfaces") and `specs/iri-mapping/spec.md`.
   - `CanonicalNTriplesObject`, `BlankNodeID`: consumed by `sparql` BFO/CCO projection chain folding (Task 3.2) and
     CONSTRUCT/DESCRIBE/export unfolding (Task 3.3); specified in `design.md` and `specs/iri-mapping/spec.md`.
4. **Am I asking a caller to predict something the server could observe?**
   - No; `NewMapper` normalizes trailing slashes and empty base IRIs identically to `export.SubjectIRI`, and
     `CanonicalNTriplesObject` derives RDF object classification directly from the exporter.
