## Exported-Surface Ownership Check ("Before adding anything new")

1. **Who owns this responsibility today?**
   - Entity ID, literal prefix, and six-position pattern validation and matching are owned by
     `github.com/c360studio/semengine/pkg/types` (`ValidateEntityID`, `ValidateEntityIDPrefix`,
     `ValidateEntityIDPattern`, `MatchEntityIDPattern`).
   - Entity state representation, batch response envelope (`EntityBatchResponse`, `MissingEntity`, `MissingNotFound`),
     and `ErrorCodeEntityNotFound` are owned by `github.com/c360studio/semengine/graph`.
   - Classified error matching (`*errs.ClassifiedError`, `errs.ClassifiedCode`) is owned by
     `github.com/c360studio/semengine/pkg/errs`.
   - SemEngine does not own SemSparql's planner-facing `GraphReader` interface or in-memory test fixture reader
     (`MemoryReader`).
2. **Is the premise true?**
   - Confirmed in `openspec/changes/bootstrap-semsparql-sidecar/inventory.md` and `semengine/processor/graph-ingest/query.go`.
3. **Who is it for?**
   - `GraphReader`, `MatchAllPattern`, `ErrNilContext`, `ErrEntityNotFound`, `ErrScanBudgetExceeded`: consumed by
     `sparql` BGP planner/evaluator (Tasks 2.2, 3.1–3.3), `server` `/entities/{id}.jsonld` and `/export` handlers
     (Task 3.3), and `NATSRPCReader` (Task 4.1); specified in `design.md` ("Responsibilities and consumer surfaces")
     and `specs/bgp-planner/spec.md`.
   - `MemoryReader`, `NewMemoryReader`: consumed by `reader` unit/property/fuzz tests (Task 1.2), `sparql` and `server`
     unit/property tests (Tasks 2.2–3.3), and `TestScanPatternReaderParity` (Task 4.1).
4. **Am I asking a caller to predict something the server could observe?**
   - No; `ScanPrefix` refuses `""` explicitly and callers use `ScanPattern(ctx, MatchAllPattern, budget)` when they
     intend a bounded full-graph scan.
